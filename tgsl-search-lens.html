03 / Time travel

DATE REFINEMENT
Google's after: and before: use Google's document date signals (commonly updated or published date). They do not guarantee an exact YouTube video upload date or complete historical results.
YouTube native has no documented exact date-range URL filter. TGSL will append after:/before: as experimental query terms when selected. YouTube may ignore these. For archival date searches, switch to Google → YouTube.

Know the boundaries

This is a query builder, not a search-results scraper. Google and YouTube decide the results. Exact quotes, operators and dates may be interpreted differently by each engine.
Which search mode is best?

Google Search: researching websites, PDFs and precise text matches.

Google → YouTube: discovering historical or niche videos by title and approximate document dates.

YouTube Direct: videos, channels, playlists, duration and feature filters.

Native exact dates: a verified upload-date guarantee requires an authenticated/data API approach; not included in this lightweight file.

Why no results inside this page?

Search pages block embedding and browsers restrict cross-origin requests. Opening the query on the original website is reliable, avoids scraping and requires no paid service.

Google's allintitle: vs intitle:

intitle:term targets one title term. allintitle:word1 word2 attempts to require multiple title words, but combining allin* with other operators can have surprising behavior. TGSL uses repeatable intitle: terms for combined searches.
